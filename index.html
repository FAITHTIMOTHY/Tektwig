Dynamic Security Policy Controls

Decide WHO, WHEN, WHERE, and HOW Data is Shared. Côntrol™ is a patent-pending solution that can be used on multiple platforms, including Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android.

  • Control WHO can access your data (Identity verification)
  • Control WHEN they can access your data (time-fencing)
  • Control WHERE they can access your data (geofencing)
  • Enforce HOW your data is accessed (DRM - no forward, copy, print or save)
  • REVOKE access at any time
