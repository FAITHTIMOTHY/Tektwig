Secure File Sharing

With Côntrol, you can share sensitive documents across devices and platforms — Windows, macOS, iOS, Android — while controlling exactly who can access them, where, when, and how.

Data Rights Management

Product Demonstration

See what Côntrol™ looks like

Your data. Your Rules.

Decide WHO, WHEN, WHERE, and HOW Data is Shared. Côntrol™ is a patent-pending solution that can be used on multiple platforms, including Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. It provides the ability to:

  • Control WHO can access your data
  • Control WHEN they can access your data (time-fencing)
  • Control WHERE they can access your data (geofencing)
  • Enforce HOW your data is accessed (DRM - no forward, copy, print or save)
  • REVOKE access at any time

Côntrol™ for mobile

On the move and need to securely share your files from your mobile device? Côntrol has you covered with support for Android and iOS devices. Just like our desktop solution, you can control who, where, how, and when your data is accessed, even when sending it to someone else’s device.

Our app uses advanced encryption and multi-factor geofencing, ensuring your files are only accessible in approved locations.

Côntrol™ for desktop

Côntrol lives on multiple operating systems (OS), including your desktop OS. Whether your OS is Mac or Windows, Côntrol protects your data. Use Côntrol to keep the files on your desktop secure or create a secure file to share with others.

Côntrol™ for Outlook

Email is one of the easiest ways to use Côntrol, as it's plugin for Outlook allows you to seamlessly send secure emails and attachments to others with just a couple of simple clicks.
